# Business Intelligence: Methods and Applications - Course Materials

This repository contains all lecture slides for the course **"Business Intelligence: Methods and Applications"** offered by the School of Artificial Intelligence, Tangshan University. The slides are written in LaTeX Beamer and cover core BI methods, classical algorithms, advanced discussions, and critical thinking training.

## Course Overview

- **Course Title:** Business Intelligence: Methods and Applications  
- **Author:** Qingxin Meng (Michael)  
- **Institution:** School of Artificial Intelligence, Tangshan University  
- **Objective:** To equip students with the ability to support decision-making using data analysis methods, with an emphasis on understanding algorithmic principles, the appropriate use of tools, and critical interpretation of results.

A distinctive feature of this course is **the allowance and encouragement of using AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, DeepSeek) in lab sessions**, while requiring students to understand AI-generated code and complete their reports independently. This approach shifts the key competence from *coding itself* to **problem understanding and result interpretation**.

## Syllabus

Currently uploaded chapters:

1. **Course Introduction**  
   `Introduction.pdf`  
   Course orientation, learning strategies, and guidelines for using AI tools.

2. **Association Analysis**  
   `Association-Analysis.pdf`  
   Support, confidence, lift; Apriori and FP-Growth algorithms; Simpson's paradox; transition from association analysis to probabilistic modeling.

3. **Probabilistic Modeling**  
   `Probabilistic-Modeling.pdf`  
   Information content, information entropy, maximum entropy principle; probabilistic modeling and exponential family distributions.

Subsequent chapters (classification, clustering, regression, etc.) will be uploaded gradually. Stay tuned.
